APPVIDEOAI

1. Giai nen nguyen folder, giu _internal canh AppVideoAI.exe.
2. Mo AppVideoAI.exe.
3. Chon ZIP phim, quet tap, tich chon tap va xuat video.

Du lieu phim khong di kem ban phan mem nay.

DICH PHU DE 10 NGON NGU

1. Mo Cai dat NVIDIA + Beeknoee. Nhap NVIDIA API key.
   NVIDIA: https://build.nvidia.com/nvidia/riva-translate-4b-instruct-v2
   Beeknoee cho Filipino/du phong: https://platform.beeknoee.com/dashboard
2. Bam Thu NVIDIA, nhap va thu Beeknoee neu can Filipino/du phong, sau do Luu va dong. Key duoc ma hoa tren tai khoan Windows nay.
3. Quet phim, tich tap va ngon ngu. Nguon SRT phai la tieng Anh.
   Neu ZIP co san ngon ngu da chon, app se lay truc tiep va khong goi API.
4. Bam Tao SRT / tiep tuc de tao phu de truoc. Mo SRT cua tap de xem/sua loi thoai.
5. Bam Xuat video de dich (neu can) va xuat phim voi ngon ngu da chon.

Hai ban = 1 video clean moi tap + video burn cho moi ngon ngu da tich.
Subtitles/vi = tieng Viet; subtitles/tl = Filipino/Tagalog; burn = video gan chu.
Sua SRT roi xuat lai se tao video _v2, _v3... va giu video cu.
Giu file .translation.json va .export.json de tranh dich/xuat lai khong can thiet.
Neu mat mang, bam lai cung nut va giu nguyen noi luu de tiep tuc.

NVIDIA mien phi dich 9 ngon ngu; Beeknoee dich Filipino va du phong khi NVIDIA loi.
Hai dich vu chi nhan loi thoai, khong nhan video. Free tier co the bi gioi han toc do/han muc.
Ban dich can ra soat ten rieng, cach xung ho va ngu canh truoc khi phat hanh.
